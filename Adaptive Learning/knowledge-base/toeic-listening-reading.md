# TOEIC Listening and Reading: scope and structure

## Scope

This note covers the TOEIC Listening and Reading test, not TOEIC Speaking and Writing or TOEIC Bridge. It summarizes ETS public test information. It does not reproduce official test questions or claim that the practice content in this repository is ETS-authored.

## What the test measures

ETS describes Listening and Reading as an assessment of English listening and reading skills used in workplace contexts. Scenarios may resemble communication in everyday professional settings, but candidates are assessed on language comprehension; specialist business experience should not be assumed as a prerequisite.

## Sections and parts

The multiple-choice test has two timed sections, each with 100 questions:

- Listening, about 45 minutes: Part 1 Photographs; Part 2 Question-Response; Part 3 Conversations; Part 4 Talks.
- Reading, about 75 minutes: Part 5 Incomplete Sentences; Part 6 Text Completion; Part 7 Reading Comprehension.

ETS states that the full session takes about 2.5 hours when biographical questions are included. Check the current ETS materials before using timing or format details in a live product because test policies and delivery details can change.

## Practice content guidance

- Listening items should be linked to the audio segment they test; questions about a conversation should not be answerable from a detached transcript alone.
- Reading items should include the complete sentence or passage context needed to justify the answer.
- Workplace contexts can include communications such as notices, schedules, email, conversations, and short talks, but vocabulary difficulty should test English comprehension rather than obscure industry knowledge.
- Do not convert raw practice accuracy into an official TOEIC score without a validated, appropriately calibrated scoring method.
- Label locally written material as original practice content, not an official TOEIC test question.
